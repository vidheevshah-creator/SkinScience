THE BASICS

What is it?

Benzoyl peroxide is a skincare ingredient used mainly to treat acne. It is a topical antimicrobial agent that helps reduce acne-causing bacteria in the skin. It is commonly used by people treating acne breakouts.

THE SCIENCE

How does it work?

Benzoyl peroxide can enter the hair follicles and break down to release reactive oxygen. This oxygen helps oxidize bacterial proteins, damaging acne-associated bacteria. This reduces the amount of acne-causing bacteria in the follicles and helps calm acne.

It also has effects that can help reduce clogged pores, but its main role is antimicrobial.

WHERE IT ACTS

What does it affect?

Benzoyl peroxide acts mainly in the skin and hair follicles. It targets acne-associated bacteria and can affect the surrounding skin, sometimes causing dryness or irritation.

It can also bleach hair and dyed fabrics, including towels, pillowcases, and clothing.

USES

What is it used for?

Benzoyl peroxide is used to treat mild to moderate acne. It is available in washes, gels, creams, and lotions. It may be used alone or as part of a treatment plan with other acne medicines.

LOCATION

Where does it work?

Benzoyl peroxide works on the skin, especially within hair follicles and pores where acne-associated bacteria and buildup are found.

04

WHO USES IT?

Who uses it?

Benzoyl peroxide is commonly used by people treating acne breakouts, including inflamed pimples. A healthcare professional may also recommend it as part of an acne treatment plan.

ROUTINE

How is it used?

Benzoyl peroxide comes in leave-on products and rinse-off washes. The exact directions depend on the product, its concentration, and the area being treated.

  1. 01
    Start gently

    Begin with a lower concentration or less frequent use, as directed on the label.

  2. 02
    Apply as directed

    Use it on clean, dry skin if the product instructions say to do so. Wash your hands after applying.

  3. 03
    Protect fabrics

    Keep it away from hair, towels, pillowcases, and clothing because it can bleach them.

  4. 04
    Introduce one active at a time

    This makes it easier to identify what helps or irritates your skin.

SAFETY FIRST

What should you be careful about?

  • Do not apply it to broken, sunburned, or irritated skin.
  • Avoid contact with your eyes, lips, and mouth. If it gets into your eyes, rinse thoroughly with water.
  • Avoid unnecessary sun exposure and follow the product's sun-protection directions.
  • Benzoyl peroxide can bleach hair and dyed fabrics, so let it dry as directed and keep it away from towels and clothing.
  • Using too much or combining multiple irritating acne products can increase dryness and irritation. Ask a healthcare professional if you are unsure about combining treatments.

KNOW YOUR SKIN

What are the side effects?

COMMON

Mild effects

Dryness or tightness, redness, peeling, itching, stinging, or a mild burning sensation.

OVERUSE / IRRITATION

Too much, too often

More intense dryness, redness, peeling, burning, or irritation. Using extra product does not make it work faster.

ALLERGIC REACTION

Rare but important

Hives, marked swelling, blistering, or trouble breathing can indicate a serious allergic reaction. Stop using the product and seek urgent medical help; trouble breathing is an emergency.

Most users experience no serious problems, but severe irritation or allergy needs medical attention.

STORAGE

How should it be stored?

Follow the storage instructions on the packaging. Keep the product tightly closed and away from excessive heat. Do not use a product that has passed its expiration date.

THE TIMELINE

What should you expect?

SHORT TERM

Mild dryness, redness, or light peeling may occur when first starting.

AFTER SEVERAL WEEKS

Breakouts may begin to improve. It can take around 4 to 6 weeks to notice an effect, and improvement may continue with consistent use.
